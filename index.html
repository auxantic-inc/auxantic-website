FAQ.

The questions we hear most.

How quickly will I see results? +
Most clients see measurable engagement improvements within the first 30 days. Significant follower and reach growth typically becomes visible between months 2 and 3, as our content systems compound.
Do I need to provide content or ideas? +
On our premium packages, no. We take full ownership of strategy, creative direction, and execution — your only touchpoint is a monthly alignment call. On starter plans, light collaboration on direction is part of the process.
Which platforms do you manage? +
Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X (Twitter), LTK and more. The platforms we focus on depend on your business, audience and goals.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. All packages are month-to-month with no lock-in contracts. We operate on a 30-day notice policy. We believe in earning your business every month, not trapping you in a contract.
What languages do you work in? +
We work in English primarily, but our team operates across languages without friction. Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Spanish, German — content localised for each market, not just translated. No barriers, no handoffs, no delays.
What makes Auxantic different? +
Most agencies post content. We build growth engines. Every client gets a strategy built from scratch around their brand, their audience, and their goals — not a recycled template. We treat analytics as seriously as aesthetics, and we don't stop at delivery. We stay in it, we track what works, we cut what doesn't, and we push until the numbers move. That's not a service. That's a standard.
