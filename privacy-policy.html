Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 5, 2026 Last Updated: July 5, 2026 Company: Auxantic (under WAMZ Group)

Auxantic ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard the data you provide when visiting our website or engaging with our services. By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

01

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways — information you give us directly, and information collected automatically when you use our website.

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, website or social media URL, and any details you submit through our contact or booking forms.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, actions taken on-site, session duration, scroll depth, and general interaction data.
  • Device & Technical Data: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, IP address, and general geographic location derived from your IP.
  • Form Submissions: Any information you voluntarily submit via our enquiry or audit request forms, including service preferences and business details.
  • Cookies & Tracking Data: We use cookies and third-party analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our site and to improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to operate and improve our business. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Respond to enquiries and audit requests submitted through our forms.
  • Schedule discovery calls and communicate about our services.
  • Improve our website's performance, content, and user experience.
  • Understand how visitors navigate our site using aggregated, anonymised analytics data.
  • Send service-related communications where you have expressly requested them.
  • Comply with legal obligations where required.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes.

03

Analytics & Tracking Tools

We use the following third-party tools to analyse website usage and improve our services. Each tool processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): Tracks aggregated site traffic, page views, and visitor behaviour. IP anonymisation is enabled. Data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You may opt out via Google's opt-out browser add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation): Records anonymised session data including heatmaps and scroll behaviour to help us understand how users interact with our pages. Clarity does not track personally identifiable information. Data is governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.): Provides security, performance optimisation, and DDoS protection for our website. Cloudflare may process request data (including IP addresses) as part of its content delivery and security services, subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

These tools may use cookies or similar tracking technologies. You may manage cookie preferences through your browser settings at any time.

04

Form Processing & Third-Party Services

Our contact and audit request forms are processed through Formspree, a third-party form backend service. Submissions you make are securely transmitted to Formspree's servers and forwarded to our team. Formspree processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

Discovery call scheduling is facilitated through Calendly. When you book a call, Calendly may collect your name, email address, and scheduling preferences. This data is subject to Calendly's Privacy Policy.

We only share with these services the data that is strictly necessary to provide their function. We do not authorise them to use your data for any other purpose.

05

Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable and industry-standard measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. These measures include:

  • Secure HTTPS transmission across our entire website.
  • Use of trusted third-party service providers who are bound by contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Limiting access to personal data to only those team members who require it to perform their role.

While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

06

How Long We Retain Your Data

  • Form submissions and enquiry data are retained for a period necessary to respond to and fulfil your request, or as required by law.
  • Analytics data (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity) is retained in accordance with each platform's default retention settings.
  • You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at info@auxantic.com.

07

Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to enhance your experience and enable analytics. The types of cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly.
  • Analytics cookies: Used by Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to collect anonymised usage data.
  • Performance cookies: Used by Cloudflare to optimise page delivery and security.

You can disable or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

08

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@auxantic.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

09

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will take immediate steps to delete it. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us at info@auxantic.com.

10

Links to Third-Party Sites

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
